Private by design

Turn off your Wi-Fi. Lumina won't notice.

Every feature works offline, including Places. The world map is drawn from an outline bundled with the app and the reverse geocoder is built in, so no tile server ever learns where your photos were taken.

  • No Lumina account, cloud library or sync service.
  • No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting.
  • Update checks are off by default, and every update is signature-checked.
  • Export as JPEG strips every bit of metadata, GPS included.

Fast and native

Smooth at any size.

Lumina is written in Rust on GPUI, the GPU-accelerated engine behind the Zed editor. Decoding, cataloguing and search run in a separate core, so the interface never waits on your disk.

  • A virtualised grid that scrolls at up to 120 FPS on ProMotion displays.
  • Mosaic and Years / Months / Days browsing.
  • Instant zoom, pan and 1:1 pixel inspection in the viewer.

Search

Portable metadata

Your work survives Lumina.

Lumina never modifies your originals. Ratings, colour labels, keywords, captions and copyright are written to Adobe-compatible XMP sidecars next to your files, so Lightroom and other photo tools can read them. If you ever leave, everything comes with you.

  • Metadata templates for your name, copyright and contact details.
  • Imports sidecars written by other apps, and never overwrites them.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is Lumina really free?

Yes. Lumina is open source under the MIT licence. There is no paid tier, no account and no storage plan to upsell.

Does Lumina upload anything?

No. It only goes online when you choose Check for Updates (or turn on daily checks, which are off by default) or paste a link into Import Video from URL. There is no analytics, telemetry or crash reporting.

Will it change or move my files?

No. Lumina never modifies your originals. Edits are stored in its catalog and applied on screen, and metadata is written to XMP sidecars next to your files. Importing copies files into dated folders; opening a folder indexes it in place.

Can I use it alongside Lightroom or my favourite editor?

Yes. Lumina reads and writes Adobe-compatible XMP sidecars, and Edit in… (⌘E) opens any photo in the editor you choose. Lumina is a library and culling tool, not a RAW developer.

Which formats does it support?

JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, TIFF and common camera RAW files, plus MP4, MOV, M4V, AVI and MKV video. Linux does not preview HEIC or RAW yet. Format details

What about Windows?

Windows is planned. Watch the repository's releases on GitHub to hear when it lands.

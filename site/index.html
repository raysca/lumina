Questions

Frequently asked

Is Lumina really free?

Yes. Lumina is open source under the MIT licence. There is no paid tier, no account and no storage plan to upsell.

Does Lumina upload anything?

No. It only goes online when you choose Check for Updates (or turn on daily checks, which are off by default) or paste a link into Import Video from URL. There is no analytics, telemetry or crash reporting.

Will it change or move my files?

No. Lumina never modifies your originals. Edits are stored in its catalog and applied on screen, and metadata is written to XMP sidecars next to your files. Importing copies files into dated folders; opening a folder indexes it in place.

Which photo and video formats does it support?

JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, TIFF and common camera RAW files, plus MP4, MOV, M4V, AVI and MKV video. Linux does not preview HEIC or RAW yet. Format details

Can I use it with Lightroom or my favourite editor?

Yes. Lumina reads and writes Adobe-compatible XMP sidecars, and Edit in… (⌘E) opens any photo in the editor you choose. Lumina is a library and culling tool, not a RAW developer.

What about Windows?

Windows is planned. Watch the repository's releases on GitHub to hear when it lands.
